## Summary
**Fixed:** pinch-to-zoom often no-oped because the `<video>` element stole touches (esp. Safari).

## Change
- Transparent RNGH gesture overlay owns pinch/pan/double-tap
- `VideoView` / video layer `pointerEvents="none"`

## Evidence
### Before
Pinch fought Safari / native video hit-testing.

### After
Overlay receives gestures; video just renders.
