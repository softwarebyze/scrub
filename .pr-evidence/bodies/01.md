## Summary
**Fixed:** mobile Safari popping the default video play button / going fullscreen and fighting Scrub's controls.

## Change
- `playsInline`
- `fullscreenOptions={{ enable: false }}`
- keeps `nativeControls={false}`

## Evidence
Expo `VideoView.web` maps these to `<video playsInline controlsList="nofullscreen">`.

### Before
Safari owns the surface → giant play overlay, accidental fullscreen.

### After
Inline scrub surface only — Scrub chrome owns play/pause/seek.
